**About**

We are a passionate team of three game developers, who got hooked onto coding and games as teens. 
Together, we have been operating **Signal**, our very own game studio.

Over several years, we have developed several games. From thrilling space shooters like *Cyber Slayer Sufyaan*,
to suspenseful text-based adventures like *Do Not Judge*, all the way to [*Mohammed's Game*].

In 2026, we got together to create the *Triple Wave*, a collection of all three of our games at a discounted price. The bundle includes:
- Cyber Slayer Sufyaan
- Do Not Judge
- Catacombs escape

Our company's mission is to provide the world with enjoyable games. Have fun!
